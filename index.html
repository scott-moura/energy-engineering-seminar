UC Berkeley Energy Engineering Seminar

About the Seminar

The UC Berkeley Energy Engineering Seminar (ENGIN 93) brings together industry leaders, researchers, and innovators in the energy sector to share insights on sustainable energy solutions, technological advancements, and policy implications. This seminar series provides students with exposure to cutting-edge developments in energy engineering and creates networking opportunities with professionals in the field.

Course Instructor

Professor Scott Moura

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering

Professor Scott Moura specializes in control and optimization of energy systems, with a focus on batteries, electric vehicles, and renewable energy integration. His research aims to develop sustainable energy solutions through innovative engineering approaches.
